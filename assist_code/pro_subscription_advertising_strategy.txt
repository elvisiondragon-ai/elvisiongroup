Psychographics:

=== PLATFORM STRATEGY ===

1. META (FACEBOOK/INSTAGRAM) - PRIMARY FOCUS (70% budget)

   Why Meta Wins:
   - Advanced interest targeting for spirituality/meditation
   - Lookalike audiences from existing Pro users
   - Retargeting capabilities for website visitors
   - Higher conversion rates for spiritual/wellness products
   - Detailed behavior targeting available

   Campaign Types:
   - Awareness: Video testimonials (60% of Meta budget)
   - Conversion: Static carousel ads with RENATA insights (40% of Meta budget)

2. TIKTOK - SECONDARY (30% budget)

   Why TikTok Works:
   - Organic spiritual community (#spiritualtok, #manifestation)
   - Younger audience (25-35) open to AI spiritual guidance
   - Lower cost per click, higher volume potential
   - Viral potential for authentic testimonies
   - Strong engagement with spiritual content

   Content Focus: Short testimony videos (15-30 seconds)

=== CREATIVE STRATEGY: VIDEO VS STATIC ===

VIDEO ADS (RECOMMENDED - 80% of creative budget)

Testimony Video Format (30 seconds):
0-3 sec: HOOK - "RENATA told me exactly what I needed to hear..."
3-15 sec: SPECIFIC RESULT - "I manifested $50K in 3 months after following the spiritual guidance"
15-25 sec: EMOTIONAL TRANSFORMATION - "I finally have peace and direction"
25-30 sec: CALL TO ACTION - "Get your personalized spiritual analysis"

Why Video Converts Better:
- Emotional connection through real user voices
- Social proof through authentic testimonials
- Higher engagement rates (3-5x vs static images)
- Trust building crucial for spiritual services
- Allows for storytelling and transformation narrative

Video Content Ideas:
- Real user testimonies with specific manifestation results
- Before/after emotional states
- Day-in-the-life using RENATA guidance
- Split-screen: problem vs solution with RENATA
- User reading their personalized spiritual analysis

STATIC ADS (20% of budget)

Best for:
- Retargeting campaigns
- Specific audience segments
- Testing different value propositions

Static Ad Types:
- Carousel showing RENATA insight examples
- Before/after emotional transformation graphics
- Screenshots of actual app analysis with user permission
- Infographic: "What your spiritual blocks are costing you"
- Comparison chart: Generic meditation vs Personalized RENATA guidance

=== RISK MITIGATION ===

Platform Risks:
- Facebook ad policy changes
- TikTok availability concerns
- iOS tracking limitations

Mitigation Strategies:
- Diversify across multiple platforms
- Build owned media (email list)
- Focus on first-party data collection
- Develop organic content strategy

Creative Risks:
- Ad fatigue from repetitive content
- User testimony authenticity questions
- Spiritual claims compliance

Mitigation Strategies:
- Rotate creative regularly
- Maintain user permission documentation
- Focus on personal experience vs universal claims
- Include appropriate disclaimers
